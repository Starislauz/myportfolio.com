01 · Profile

About me

Backend fundamentals, applied to machine learning. I care about what happens after the model works — validation, deployment, observability, and what it costs to run.

I'm Anthony Njoku, an AI, MLOps and Backend Engineer who builds systems that solve real problems rather than demos that stay in a notebook.

My work centres on AI and MLOps pipelines — integrating machine learning models, automating the path to production, and keeping deployments observable and scalable. I write services in Python (FastAPI, Flask) and Node.js (Express), and I've shipped larger platforms in PHP & Laravel.

On the data side I work with MySQL and PostgreSQL; on the infrastructure side with Docker, Kubernetes, MLflow and CI/CD on AWS and Azure. That covers API design, automated training runs, real-time analytics, and monitoring of models once they're live.

01 AI MLOps

RAG document chat API

A production RAG service over your own documents. Hybrid retrieval (vector + BM25) with cross-encoder re-ranking, and a strict abstention path: if the answer isn't in the uploads it says so instead of guessing. Deployed on infrastructure I run myself — Docker Compose behind Nginx on Oracle Cloud, with Cloudflare handling DNS, CDN and TLS for the custom domain. A 30-question golden set scores correctness, faithfulness and abstain accuracy on every change.

02 AI Automation

Telegram market analyst bot

A Rust service backing a Telegram bot: an AI analyst for SOL, BTC, ETH and BNB that reads Bybit perpetuals across three timeframes — funding, open interest, order-book depth, VWAP, EMA200 and clustered support and resistance with touch counts. Signals must pass hard risk rules or they are discarded, and every one is tracked to target or stop with the hit rate published. It never places an order.
